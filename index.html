Abstract

Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) achieve photorealistic novel view synthesis but typically require dense multi-view captures. DA4NeRF addresses this limitation through a depth-guided data augmentation strategy that synthesizes additional training views from estimated depth maps, significantly reducing the number of input images required while maintaining high reconstruction quality across diverse scenes.
